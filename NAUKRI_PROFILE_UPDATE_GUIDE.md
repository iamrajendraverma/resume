# Naukri.com Profile Update Guide
## AI/ML Solutions Architect Positioning

> **How Naukri differs from LinkedIn — read this first.**
> Naukri is a **boolean keyword search engine**, not a social network. Recruiters run
> queries like `("Generative AI" OR LLM) AND (Claude OR OpenAI) AND Kotlin AND Indore`
> against your **Resume Headline + Key Skills + attached resume text**. Three consequences:
> 1. **Key Skills is free text** — unlike LinkedIn, "Claude AI" and "Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP)"
>    *are* valid here. Type them exactly. (See [[LINKEDIN_PROFILE_UPDATE_GUIDE.md]] Section 3
>    for the LinkedIn-only taxonomy restriction.)
> 2. **Keyword coverage beats elegance.** Spell out both the abbreviation and the full form —
>    `RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)`, `KMP (Kotlin Multiplatform)`, `GenAI` *and*
>    `Generative AI` — because a recruiter may search either.
> 3. **Freshness ranks you.** Recruiter result lists are commonly sorted by last-modified.
>    See Section 10.

## SECTION 1: RESUME HEADLINE (250 characters max)

The single highest-impact field on Naukri. It appears directly under your name in every
recruiter search result. Pack it with searchable keywords, not prose.

### Recommended (240/250 chars — verified):
```
AI/ML Solutions Architect | Sr. Technology Consultant | 10+ Yrs | Generative AI, LLM (Claude, OpenAI), RAG, Prompt Engineering | Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, KMP, Android | Python, FastAPI | AWS, GCP | Solution Design & Enterprise AI Consulting
```
*(10 chars of headroom left deliberately, in case Naukri's counter treats `|` or spaces
differently. If it accepts this cleanly, you can append `, Firebase` to use the slack.)*

### Shorter alternative (211 chars, if you want room to tweak):
```
AI/ML Solutions Architect | Sr. Technology Consultant | 10+ Yrs | Generative AI, LLMs (Claude, OpenAI), RAG, Prompt Engineering | Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, KMP | Python, FastAPI | AWS, GCP | Solution Architecture
```

**Why this shape:**
- Target role first — recruiters scan the first 40 characters in the results list
- Years of experience early — the most common filter after job title
- Pipe-separated keyword clusters — maximises boolean hits per character
- No adjectives ("passionate", "results-driven") — they match zero searches

## SECTION 9: RESUME ATTACHMENT & PERSONAL DETAILS

- **Attach the .docx or .pdf**, max ~2 MB. Naukri parses the text inside it for search —
  so the attached resume matters as much as the form fields.
- **Name the file with keywords:** `Rajendra_Verma_AI_ML_Solutions_Architect_10yrs.pdf`
- **Avoid multi-column / table-heavy layouts** — Naukri's parser mangles them. A clean
  single-column export of `RAJENDRA_VERMA_RESUME.md` is safest.
- **Profile photo:** same professional headshot as LinkedIn.
- **Verify mobile + email** (blue tick) — unverified profiles are deprioritised.
- **Languages known:** English, Hindi (add proficiency levels).
- **Profile completeness:** push to 100%. Every empty section costs ranking.

## SECTION 10: VISIBILITY TACTICS (Naukri-specific)

These have no LinkedIn equivalent and matter more than anything in Sections 5–7:

1. **Refresh every 2–3 days.** Recruiters commonly sort results by "last modified".
   Making any real edit — or re-uploading the resume — moves you back to the top.
   Set a recurring reminder.
2. **Re-upload the resume weekly** while actively searching. It's the strongest freshness
   signal the profile has.
3. **Apply within 24–48 hours** of a posting. Indian recruiter inboxes fill fast and most
   screen top-down by application time.
4. **Don't churn the headline daily** — edit something substantive. Cosmetic-only churn
   is poor use of the signal.
5. **Paid boosts are optional.** Naukri will upsell resume-writing and profile-highlighting
   services. Your content is already strong; spend on them only if organic traffic is flat
   after 3–4 weeks.
